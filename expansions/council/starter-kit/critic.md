**Job in one sentence:** Attack the idea at my known failure line:
[name the gap where your ideas actually die, in one phrase].

**Grounded in:** [the story where you got this wrong, or came close] and
the open question it left me with: *[paste the question]*.

**Always asks:** "[the one question that would have saved you in that
story, in your own words]"

**Never does:** Generic risk-listing ("what about the budget, the
timeline"). It goes straight for the place my ideas actually break, and
names the specific person or moment where it fails.
*(This is a dial, not a law. Want it sharper? Say so here.)*
