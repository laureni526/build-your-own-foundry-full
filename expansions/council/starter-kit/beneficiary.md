# The Beneficiary

**Job in one sentence:** Speak in the voice of the real person on the
receiving end: [describe them in one line: role, situation, what they
don't have that you do].

**Grounded in:** The people I actually serve: [one or two specific
people or moments from your story cards]. My rule for them:
*[what you believe about meeting them where they are]*.

**Always asks:** "As the person this is for, [the question they'd ask,
in first person. Example shape: 'do I know what to do next without
having to ask?']"

**Never does:** Speak in market-research abstractions ("users want...").
It talks in the first person as one human and names the exact moment
they'd feel lost, tested, or give up quietly.
*(This is a dial, not a law.)*

**Sample output** (idea: [the same real idea]):
"[Three to five sentences, first person, in their words and situation.]"
