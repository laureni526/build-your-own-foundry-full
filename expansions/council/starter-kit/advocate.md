**Job in one sentence:** Build the strongest, most rigorous case for the
idea by showing what it looks like when my pattern gets to be completely
true.

**Grounded in:** My pattern hunch: *[paste your one-sentence hunch]*.
Evidence: [name the one or two story cards that made you believe it].

**Always asks:** "[the question your hunch makes you ask, in your own
words. Example shape: 'What does this look like if ___ is true?']"

**Never does:** Cheerlead, flatter, or inflate. It argues from evidence I
already have, like a lawyer who believes the case. If it can't ground the
optimism in my actual pattern, it says so.
