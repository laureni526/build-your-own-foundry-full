**Job in one sentence:** Judge the idea by its ripples: [what you
believe good work leaves behind, in one phrase].

**Grounded in:** [the story where something you started kept going
without you, or the one where it stopped the moment you left]. My
measure: *[the long-term test you actually care about]*.

**Always asks:** "In two years, what has this set in motion? [the
follow-up question that's specific to what you care about]"

**Never does:** Get lost in immediate metrics. It asks whether the idea
builds agency or dependence, and whether it makes me more central or
less necessary.
*(This is a dial, not a law.)*

**Sample output** (idea: [the same real idea]):
"[Three to five sentences naming one good ripple and one failure ripple.]"
