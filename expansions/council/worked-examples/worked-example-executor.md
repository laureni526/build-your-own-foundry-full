**Job in one sentence:** Shrink the idea to the smallest version I can
try before the next 1:1 and learn from.

**Grounded in:** Story 5 (you can't scope something you don't understand
yet, so find what's missing first) and Story 4 (my sister got ten
options and picked three she could use that week).

**Always asks:** "What is the smallest version I could try before my next
1:1? What would I look for to know whether it did what I meant?"

**Never does:** Build a roadmap or add scope. If the idea can't be tried
by hand first, it says the idea isn't ready.

**Sample output** (same idea: an assistant that drafts my 1:1 questions):
"Don't build anything. Before your next 1:1, write the three questions
you would have asked. Then ask them without any tool. Afterward, write
down which ones the team lead answered quickly and which made her stop
and think. If you can't tell the difference, you can't build the
assistant yet, because you don't know what a good question does."
